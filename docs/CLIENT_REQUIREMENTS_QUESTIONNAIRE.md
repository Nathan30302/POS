# NEXORA — Quick questions for your shop

Please answer these so we can set up the system for you.  
Short answers are fine. Skip anything you’re unsure about.

---

### About your business

1. What is the **shop / company name** (as it should appear on the screen and receipts)?
2. Do you have a **logo**? If yes, please send the file (PNG or SVG).
3. Preferred **colours** for the app — or should we match the **logo colours**?
4. Shop **phone number** and **address** to show on receipts? (optional)
5. Is this for **one shop only**, or will you need it for **more than one shop / branch** later?

---

### Products & prices

6. Roughly how many products do you sell? (e.g. about 50 / 100 / 300+)
7. Can you send a simple **product list** (WhatsApp / Excel) with: name, selling price, and stock if you know it?
8. Do your products already have **barcodes**, or should we help you **create and print** barcodes?
9. Main product groups / categories you use (e.g. pens, books, paper, art, office)?

---

### Selling & payments

10. How do customers usually pay? (Cash, Airtel Money, MTN, Zamtel, card, other — list all you use)
11. Do you ever give **discounts** at the till? (Yes / No). If yes, who is allowed to give them?
12. Do you want receipts by: **print**, **WhatsApp**, or **both**?

---

### People using the system

13. Who will use it? (e.g. owner + 2 cashiers)
14. Do you want an **Admin / Owner** who can manage everything, and **Staff** with limited access?
15. As Admin, which things should Staff **not** be able to do?  
    (examples: see cost prices, change prices, delete products, cancel sales, view full reports)
16. Should each person have their own **login**, or is one shared till login OK for now?

---

### How you’ll use it day to day

17. Main device at the counter: **laptop**, **tablet**, or **phone**?
18. Will you scan products with a **barcode scanner / phone**, or mostly search by name?
19. Do you need **low stock warnings** when items are running out? (Yes / No)
20. Anything else that is **must-have** for you, in one sentence?

---

### Please send (if you have them)

- Logo file  
- Product list (even a rough one)  
- Example of how you write sales today (photo is fine)

---

Thank you — once we have this, we can customise NEXORA to your shop.
