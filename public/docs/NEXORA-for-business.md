# NEXORA for business owners

**A short introduction to the shop operating system**

---

## What NEXORA is

NEXORA is a shop operating system. It brings your till, stock, and owner desk into one place so the counter and the back office stay aligned.

You sell on Sell. Stock updates as you sell. Owners and managers use Command to see what is moving — and what needs attention. Receipts carry your shop name, not a generic label.

## Who it is for

NEXORA is built for busy counters: retail shops, pharmacies, grocery stores, and multi-branch businesses that need the till to work when the day is loud and the network is not always kind.

If you run one shop or several, and you care that sales, stock, and staff access stay under control, this is for you.

## How it helps

**Sales and stock stay in sync.** When an item leaves the counter, the shelf count moves with it. You spend less time reconciling “what we sold” with “what we still have.”

**Receipts with your shop name.** Customers see your business on every receipt — phone and address when you add them.

**Roles for staff.** Cashiers can sell. Managers can adjust stock, voids, and discounts when you allow it. Each login opens only the tools that person is meant to use.

**Command for leaks and trust.** Owners get a clear desk: sales, stock pressure, and signals that something may be off — so you can trust the floor without standing over every sale.

**Phone till and offline resilience.** Sell from a phone or tablet. When the connection drops, the till can keep working and catch up when you are back online.

**Multi-branch ready.** Run more than one location under the same business, with a shared way of selling and checking in.

## How you use it (day to day)

1. **Create a shop** — Sign up and register your business.
2. **Get approved** — A NEXORA admin reviews and activates the shop before the till goes live.
3. **Set your name** — Put your shop name (and contact details) on receipts in Settings.
4. **Add products** — Build your catalogue so the till knows prices and stock.
5. **Sell** — Open a shift → scan or pick items → take payment → Complete the sale.
6. **Check Command** — See how the day is going and spot issues early.
7. **Close the shift** — End cleanly so cash and sales match what happened on the floor.

Training lives in the product: open **How to use** anytime for a full walkthrough of the real screens.
